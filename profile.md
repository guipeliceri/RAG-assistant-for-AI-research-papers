# Interest profile

Advances in current frontier models: new large language model releases and technical reports,
architectures (transformers, mixture of experts, state space models), scaling laws, pretraining data
and recipes, post-training (instruction tuning, RLHF, preference optimization, reinforcement learning
for reasoning), reasoning and chain-of-thought, long context, multimodal and omni models, efficiency
(distillation, quantization, fast inference), and benchmarks that measure the capabilities and limits
of state-of-the-art models.
